Classroom Vision

"All About Me" Wall Magazine & Graduation Manifestation

To start the academic year off with excitement and vision, I photographed my students and digitally edited their pictures to add graduation hats! This was a fun way to manifest success and encourage them from day one.

I printed these special portraits for our classroom "All About Me" wall magazine. Each student glued their picture down and wrote about themselves—including their names, ages, hobbies, and things they love. The students were incredibly happy and proud of their work!

I made sure every student did the project completely by themselves. By keeping this on our classroom wall all year, we could look back at the end of the semester and physically see the amazing progress they had made in their writing skills.

An amazing cultural exchange experience bridging Morocco and the United States! I connected with an English teacher in the USA to organize a letter penpal writing project and a cultural box exchange between our classes.

My students had a wonderful time preparing our local cultural box to send over, and they were thrilled to receive letters, postcards, and wonderful gifts sent back by their American penpals.

Activities like this foster global awareness, cross cultural knowledge, and authentic communication skills for our learners.

Classroom Strategy

Vocabulary Memory Match Game

One of my favorite ways to either kick off or wrap up a vocabulary lesson! We play a Flashcard Memory Matching game covering all the English words we have learned throughout the year.

The cards are placed face down on the students desks. Half of the cards feature images, while the other half have the corresponding vocabulary words written on them. Students take turns flipping two cards at a time, trying to find the matching image and word pair.

If they guess right, they win the cards; if they guess wrong, they flip them back over. It is a highly engaging, fun way to reinforce memory skills and review vocabulary!
